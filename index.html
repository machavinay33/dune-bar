Our Story

An Oasis in the Heart of Jubilee Hills

Born from resilience and a shared vision, Dune Bar & Kitchen is the realization of a dream by three close friends. In 2020, amidst the challenges of the COVID-19 pandemic, they chose to leave their corporate careers behind to build something extraordinary. What started as a bold leap of faith has grown into one of Hyderabad's most successful and beloved rooftop destinations.

Step into a world where the timeless allure of ancient civilizations converges with contemporary culinary artistry. Nestled atop Apurupa Towers in the vibrant Jubilee Hills, our rooftop sanctuary offers breathtaking views and an atmosphere where Egyptian-inspired architecture meets modern indulgence.
